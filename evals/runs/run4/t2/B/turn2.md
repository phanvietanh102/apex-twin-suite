## How it deals with what happened last spring

You were off sick, and the PI limitation date was only in your diary. With the register:

- **No date can live only in one diary.** Every date goes on the register the same day. You simply email Priya: "Key date: matter, what, date, how worked out." Your paper diary becomes a copy, never the only place.
- **Every date has two people.** One works it out and a *different* person checks it. Until then it shows NOT CHECKED and appears in every morning email.
- **Every open matter has a backup person,** and the register flags any matter without one. While you're on sabbatical, it also flags matters whose backup is *you*, so that David's own matters get someone else.
- **Everyone sees everything.** The 8:00 email goes to all five of you. If David had been off instead of you, Priya and Sue would still have seen that PI date, months before the deadline. Limitation dates warn 6 months ahead and become "act now" at 3 months.
- **Trusted, not just installed.** In the week of 1–5 February you "go away" for a week. David runs everything from the register, and on the Friday we compare your diary with it. Any date found only in the diary is a fault we fix before March.
